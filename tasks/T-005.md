## Goal

The root screen: the fleet. `DeviceListViewModel` already does the work (Bonjour discovery
merged with `ManualDeviceStore`'s saved hosts, `/status` polled every 2s across all of them) —
this is the view over it, plus the sheet for adding a device by hand when discovery misses it.

Replaces the placeholder root view from T-001.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `DeviceListView` lists every device with live status (bpm, peers, playing)
- [ ] Discovered and manually-added devices are visually distinguishable
      (`KitchenSyncDevice.addedManually`)
- [ ] Only manual devices are swipe-to-delete — a discovered device can't be "removed",
      it just isn't there next browse
- [ ] `AddDeviceView` takes a hostname or IP and calls `addManualDevice(host:)`
- [ ] Tapping a device pushes the detail screen (T-006)
- [ ] Calls `start()` on appear and `stop()` on disappear

## Notes

`removeManualDevices(at:)` takes `IndexSet` offsets **into the filtered manual-only list**, not
into `devices`. If the view's `ForEach` is over all devices, the offsets will not line up and
you will delete the wrong row. Either bind the deletable section to `devices.filter(\.addedManually)`
or change the view model's signature — don't paper over it at the call site.

Manual devices persist through `ManualDeviceStore` (UserDefaults). Discovered ones don't, by
design: they come back on the next browse.
